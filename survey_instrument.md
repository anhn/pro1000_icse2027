# Team feedback-literacy survey (May 2026)

Administered in Norwegian. English translation by the authors. "[the platform]" replaces the platform's name.
Items Q1-Q4, Q6 and Q7 used a five-point scale (higher = more of the construct); Q5 was open-ended.
Each item was followed by an open-ended request to explain or give an example.

| Item | Question | Scale anchors |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Does the team discuss feedback from [the platform] together? | five-point scale |
| Q2 | Can you describe how these discussions usually take place? | 1 = no clear process, 5 = explicit structured process |
| Q3 | How much time does the team usually spend discussing feedback before deciding what to do? | five-point scale |
| Q4 | When you challenge a suggestion from [the platform], in discussion or as a team decision, who takes the initiative? Is it always the same person? | five-point scale |
| Q5 | Has feedback from [the platform] ever led to disagreement or tension in the team? What happened? | open-ended |
| Q6 | How does it feel to resubmit after receiving critical feedback? Is it motivating, frustrating, or something else? | 1 = very frustrating, 5 = very motivating |
| Q7 | Does the team have a shared understanding of what "good" feedback is, i.e. what makes feedback worth following up? | five-point scale |
